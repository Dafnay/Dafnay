## 👩‍💻 About me

I'm a full stack developer with a frontend focus and **2+ years building production web apps** with React, TypeScript and Laravel: CMSs, PWAs, admin dashboards and an ERP used daily by its client.

I enjoy taking products **from idea to production**, including setting up and deploying to my own VPS. I care about clean UIs, accessibility, i18n (I've shipped apps in 5 languages) and privacy-friendly design.

🏆 **1st place – Appathon Luce Gijón 2023** with an app helping vulnerable people find housing, hygiene and health resources.
